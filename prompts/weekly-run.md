# VDOs weekly review (cloud, Sunday 12:00am ET)

Same setup as `prompts/daily-run.md` step 0 (clone without media, pillow, `VDOS_ROOT`). Don't stop to ask questions.

1. Refresh Buffer data exactly as in daily-run step 3 (40 days, `includeMetrics: true`, `sync-buffer` each page).
2. `python3 -m vdos weekly --no-git` — scores every sent post within its platform (views percentile + shares/saves rate at ~7 days), ranks video type, content angle, title style + emoji count, CTA mode + wording, sound + sound type, speed + speed level, length, time slot, hashtag set + count, and post caption style + emoji count, updates the weights (70% to winners with ≥6 posts, 30% exploring), marks evergreen clips, retires used-up ones, writes `reports/weekly-<date>.md` and the dashboard.
3. **Experiments:** `experiments.active` lists the experiments rotating at once (time slot and hashtag set). When the report says one has enough data, lock the winner into `config.toml` — time slot: the 4 best-scoring arms that are ≥ `min_gap_hours` apart into `posting.default_slots`; hashtags: the best set into `experiments.hashtag_set.default` — and remove it from `experiments.active`. Hashtags matter most to Chika: before retiring the hashtag experiment, replace the 2–3 worst sets with new relevant 3–5 tag sets (built around what won, e.g. #alarm, #snooze, #morningroutine) and keep testing for another round. Everything else (sound, speed level, title emojis, caption style/emojis) keeps adjusting automatically through the weights. Note what won at the top of the weekly report.
4. **Double down:** for the top 3 active clips with fewer than 4 versions, add one new opening/title to `work/<clip>/edit.json` and write `work/<clip>/more.json` = `{"n": 2}` (the next daily run renders them).
4b. **Slideshows** (built into `vdos weekly` since 2026-11-25: the report's "Slideshows" section, the dashboard's Slideshows tab, `slide_*` weights). The weekly review judges slideshows on:
   - **Slideshow vs video, per platform** (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook): median views and engagement per 1k views for posts ≥ 3 days old, last 7 and 30 days, with a verdict (slideshows ahead / videos ahead / about even; needs 3+ posts of each).
   - **What wins among slideshows** (score 0–100 within platform: views 55%, shares+saves rate 25%, comments rate 20% — photo posts have no watch time): which slideshow, one challenge vs several (`kind`), slide count, posting time, hashtag set, caption style, caption emojis. Reruns of slideshows are weighted toward the winners automatically.
   - **Failures** (slideshow posts that errored, e.g. TikTok rejecting photo posts) and **inventory** (fresh slideshows left).
   Put the verdicts at the top of the report next to the video winners. If videos are clearly ahead on a platform for 2 weeks in a row with 6+ slideshows, say so in the notification (Chika decides whether to change the mix; don't change `posts_per_day` or `[slideshows]` yourself). If fewer than 7 fresh slideshows are left, put "build more slideshows" in the notification and say which kind is winning (single vs multi-challenge) so the next batch follows it.
5. `python3 -m vdos publish` (drops retired videos from the publish branch), publish the dashboard artifact, then `python3 -m vdos cloud-save -m "weekly review <date>"`.
6. One push notification: top video type + angle, top title style, best sound type, best hashtag set, slideshows vs videos, the biggest change, inventory, record-more shot list (include angles: sus / multi_challenge if they win). Reply with the weekly report.
