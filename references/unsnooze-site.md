# Unsnooze website (reference)

Source: https://www.tryunsnooze.com/ (read 2026-11-28). Chika: "this is a good ref to hold onto." Use it as the source of truth for
what the app does, what to call things and which numbers are real, in captions and in animation scripts. Re-read the live page if
something here looks out of date.

## How the site says it
- Page title: "Unsnooze: The alarm you can't snooze through"
- Headline: "Stop snoozing and start your day"
- Subheadline: "Complete physical and mental challenges to turn off your alarm"
- In short: an iPhone alarm that won't stop until you finish a wake-up challenge; by the time you're done, you're awake.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. Set your alarm: pick a time and a sound, choose up to 4 challenges to do in a row.
2. Finish the challenge(s): completing every one is the only way to turn the alarm off.
3. You're up: out of bed, awake, your streak grows.

## Challenges: "16 ways to wake up" (each has its own settings, e.g. how many push-ups, how hard the math)
- Body (8): Push-ups, Squats, 67 (bounce your hands up and down), Walking (walk a set distance), Stair Climb, Shake, House Hunt
  (walk around and find the target object), Barcode Scan.
- Brain (8): Math, Memory (repeat a sequence of taps), Matching (pairs of cards), Unscramble, Typing (type out a sentence),
  Tap Pattern (feel the vibration, tap it back), Cup Shuffle, Affirmation (say it out loud).

## Features ("Built to actually get you up")
- Rings until you're really up: dismiss it from the lock screen, close the app or lock the phone mid-challenge and it rings again.
- Snooze on your terms: snoozing can be allowed or turned off per alarm (how long, how many times); the challenge resumes where you left off.
- Streaks: wake-up streak, achievements, statistics.
- 25+ loud alarm sounds; import a song or record your own message.
- Emergency Stop exists for real emergencies (that morning doesn't count toward the streak).
- Explore tab > "Try Challenges": try any challenge without setting an alarm.

## Facts to respect in videos and captions
- iPhone only, iOS 26 or later (uses Apple's AlarmKit). Never imply Android.
- Rings on the lock screen, through Silent mode and Focus, even with the app closed.
- The site shows no price, rating, review or user count: don't state any.
- Say "16 ways to wake up" when giving the count in words (older videos and the App Store screenshot in the download card say "15+").
- Snooze is optional, not impossible: prefer "you can't just tap it off" / "it rings until you finish" over "there is no snooze".
